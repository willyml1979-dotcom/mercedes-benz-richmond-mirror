Some cars are designed to blend in. Others are built to make every drive something special. The 2022 Audi S3 Progressiv quattro is the perfect example of a performance sedan that delivers excitement, sophistication, and everyday practicality without compromise.

With a no-accident history, this Audi S3 offers an exciting opportunity to experience German engineering, premium craftsmanship, and the unmistakable thrill of Audi's legendary S performance lineup.

Beneath its sleek exterior sits a powerful 2.0L turbocharged four-cylinder engine producing an impressive 306 horsepower, paired with a lightning-fast 7-speed S tronic dual-clutch transmission. Audi's renowned quattro all-wheel-drive system provides exceptional traction and confidence, whether you're navigating Vancouver's rainy streets, cruising through Langley, or enjoying the winding roads along the Sea-to-Sky Highway.

The S3 isn't just about straight-line speed. Its sport-tuned suspension, precise steering, and responsive handling create a driving experience that feels connected, engaging, and incredibly rewarding. Every corner becomes an opportunity to appreciate the engineering behind Audi's performance heritage.

Step inside and discover a beautifully crafted interior that combines contemporary luxury with a true driver-focused atmosphere. The Progressiv trim offers premium finishes, heated front sport seats, Audi's Virtual Cockpit digital instrument display, an advanced MMI touchscreen infotainment system, and seamless smartphone connectivity.

On the outside, the S3 makes its presence known with aggressive S-specific styling, a bold Singleframe grille, signature LED lighting, sporty alloy wheels, and distinctive quad exhaust outlets. It's refined enough for a business meeting yet athletic enough to turn heads wherever it goes.

What makes the Audi S3 truly special is its ability to deliver sports-car excitement in a practical, premium sedan. You get exhilarating turbocharged performance, the year-round confidence of quattro all-wheel drive, and the comfort and technology expected from one of Germany's most respected luxury brands.

Whether you're looking to elevate your daily commute or simply want a vehicle that puts a smile on your face every time you start the engine, the S3 delivers an experience that goes beyond ordinary transportation.
